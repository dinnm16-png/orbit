# ORBIT — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-24_

ORBIT is an offline game. **It does not collect, store on any server, share or
sell any personal data.**

- **No accounts, no sign-in.**
- **No internet access:** the Android app does not even request the INTERNET
  permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
- **No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.**
- **Stored on your device only:** your best score and your settings (sound,
  vibration, planet numbers). They stay on your device and are deleted when you
  uninstall the game.
- **Vibration permission (Android):** used only for short haptic feedback during
  play. It gives the app no access to any data.

Children: because ORBIT collects no data at all, it collects no data from
children.

Changes: if this ever changes (for example, if online leaderboards are added),
this policy will be updated **before** the change ships, and the store listings
will be updated accordingly.

Contact: open an issue at the project's GitHub repository.
